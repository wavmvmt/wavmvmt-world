Find a way through.

Navigate a tiny virtual movement space. Plan a route around closed tiles, visit three learning stations, and reach the destination. A game of strategy and spatial thinking—not a real parkour tutorial.

Practice zone

Focused grid: use arrow keys. Or tap the direction buttons below. Dark tiles are closed; you cannot travel through them.

The three checkpoints

Your mission: Reach the Warm-up, Adapt and Wayfinding stations, in any order, then finish at the flag in the top-right corner. Closed virtual tiles require you to reroute.
